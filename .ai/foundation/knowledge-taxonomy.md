# Knowledge Taxonomy

15-branch hierarchy for knowledge classification:

1. Foundation — root principles
2. Organization — structure and roles
3. Runtime — execution directives
4. Executive — per-role knowledge
5. Domain — business domains
6. Decision — decision history
7. Framework — analytical frameworks
8. Mental Model — reasoning patterns
9. Playbook — operational playbooks
10. Experience — accumulated experience
11. Evidence — supporting evidence
12. Temporary — ephemeral context
13. External — external references
14. User — user preferences
15. Conversation — chat history
